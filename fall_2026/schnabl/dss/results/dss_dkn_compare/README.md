Columns: (1) aggregate deposit spread, levels; (2) changes; (3) aggregate total expense rate, changes; (4) mean bank cumulative beta, 1984--2021Q2; (5) same, 1984--2017; (6) published DSS 0.345.

Columns 4--5 sum current and three lag coefficients, require 60 usable regression observations and winsorize bank betas at 5%. Their N is banks. No standard errors are supplied for mean bank betas or the published benchmark. The comparable expense beta in columns 1--2 is mean D/A minus the spread slope; this conversion assumes a roughly constant deposit share. Column 3 switches to total expense and quarterly average assets together. Average assets are approximated by adjacent quarter-end assets. DKN commercial-bank screens are held fixed, so column 5 need not reproduce DSS exactly.
